# Origin

The metamodel diagram, exported from the Mermaid text in `specs/metamodel.md`.

| File | Theme | Source |
|---|---|---|
| metamodel-light.png | Light, for the White theme | https://mermaid.live |
| metamodel-dark.png | Dark, for the Gray 100 theme | https://mermaid.live |

The text in `specs/metamodel.md` is the source, and the two images are
exports of it, one per theme, made in Mermaid Live with its light and dark
themes. When the metamodel changes, paste the text into Mermaid Live, export
each theme as PNG, and replace the file. The software carries the images so
the diagram opens without a network and in the theme the page is in.
