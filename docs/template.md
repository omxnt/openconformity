# Title

The title names the document and matches the filename. This opening text describes what the document covers and how it relates to the rest of the documentation. It runs directly under the title, with no heading of its own, and stays flat at a few sentences.

## 1. Chapter

Chapters are numbered from one and named for their content. There is no table of contents, the outline in GitHub and the editor serves that purpose.

### 1.1 Section

Sections are numbered within their chapter. Two levels of heading is the normal depth, and a document needing more is usually two documents.

## 2. References

References are always the last chapter, numbered after the content chapters. The table is left in place while empty, and rows are added as sources are cited.
