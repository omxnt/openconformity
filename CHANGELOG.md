# Changelog

Every release of openconformity is recorded here, newest first. Versions follow Semantic Versioning, and each release is tagged in the repository.

## 1.0.0-beta.1, 27 September 2026

The first public beta, open to everyone at app.openconformity.org.

- A model of the CE marking work, built from the entity types and relationships the metamodel defines.
- A tree that files the model in folders, an editor for each entity, and its relationships as a graph and as a list.
- Risk estimation by the three methods of ISO/TR 14121-2, before and after protective measures.
- Safety functions with a required performance level or safety integrity level, under EN ISO 13849-1 or EN IEC 62061.
- A library holding the Machinery Regulation and its 215 essential requirements, imported into a project.
- Diagrams drawn in draw.io, handed over only after the user consents.
- Two views. The risk assessment saves as Excel. The safety function specification saves as Markdown with its diagrams, and as an Excel register.
- Projects saved as local files, the work kept in the browser between sessions, and undo and redo throughout.
